## Takeaways applied
1. Keep registration on-site with instant confirmation and badge — a clear advantage over external forms.
2. Searchable directory and sector pages are a differentiator; populate them early as exhibitors confirm.
3. Consider adding (organiser input needed): **buyer registration / hosted-buyer programme** (Bharat Mobility), **post-show report downloads** and **shuttle/hotel info** (EV India Expo).
4. Avoid urgency banners and template residue; keep honest, structured content.
